FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on pages, features and integrations. See the plans above, or send a request for a custom quote.

How long does delivery take?

A landing page usually takes days; a full business site takes a few weeks. I’ll confirm a timeline after we discuss your needs.

How many revisions are included?

Each plan includes a set number of revision rounds, listed in the plan features. Extra rounds can be added.

Do you provide hosting?

I can set up and recommend reliable hosting, or deploy to a host you already use. Hosting costs are paid to the hosting provider.

What about the domain name?

I can help you choose and register a domain in your own name, so you always own it.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Ongoing updates, backups, fixes and improvements are available after launch.
